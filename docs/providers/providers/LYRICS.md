# Lyrics Providers

Status: P3 licensed only.

## Rule

Lyrics are copyrighted content. SongChart must not scrape, copy from search results, or generate substitute lyrics from memory.

## Integration options

- licensed API/provider such as Musixmatch under a commercial agreement;
- outbound links to authorized lyric pages;
- first-party lyrics supplied by verified rights holders under explicit terms.

## Required controls

- territory;
- display format;
- attribution;
- line-level restrictions;
- caching and deletion;
- takedown;
- synchronization rights;
- translation rights;
- search indexing restrictions.

Until a license is approved, SongChart stores only metadata such as “lyrics available externally” and an authorized outbound URL.
